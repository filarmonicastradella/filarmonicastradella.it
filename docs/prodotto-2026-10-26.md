# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary (confirmed by the user, 2026-10-26): people who could play in the Filarmonica. Teenagers who study an instrument and have nowhere to play together, adults who stopped playing years ago, beginners. They arrive on a phone, often from Instagram or word of mouth, and need to understand in seconds that this is a place where people like them play, and how to start.

Secondary: people organising an event who want a group to play; funders and public bodies checking that the association is serious; the local audience looking for the next concert.

## Product Purpose

The public site of the Filarmonica Alessandro Stradella APS, a volunteer music association in Fivizzano (Lunigiana, Tuscany). Success means new musicians asking to join (`/support/join`), then bookings, donations and audience.

## Positioning

A music society recognised in the eighteenth century (1777) that went silent for almost thirty years and was refounded for the young: in Stradella Facendo and the Streetella Band the average age is under 18, and young players sit beside musicians with decades of experience. Four ensembles under one roof: Stradella Facendo (wind orchestra), Streetella Band (street band), Orchestra Infieri (mixed orchestra), Filarmonici Dissonanti (early music). The association provides instruments and rehearsal rooms; membership is 12 euros a year. Nobody is paid, conductors included; several conductors, treated as equals.

## Operating Context

Weekly rehearsals scheduled together; concerts in theatres, churches, squares, hospitals and care homes in Lunigiana. Seats: Via Stretta 5 (legal), Via Radda 7 in Fivizzano, Circolo Fantoni in Serricciolo. Calendar from Google Calendar at build time; Instagram feed via Behold (currently failing with HTTP 402, so the gallery may be empty).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- SvelteKit static site on GitHub Pages; semantic HTML that works without CSS and JS; Italian copy, English route names.
- Membership form at `/support/join` (FormSubmit). Contact form at `/contacts/message`.
- Pages hidden as `_page.svelte` stay hidden.
- Two musicians appear only as "Andrei" and "Ricciardi" by their choice: never complete those names.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Filarmonica Alessandro Stradella" (APS only in legal contexts). Logo: the lyre under the crescent moon of Lunigiana (`src/lib/assets/favicon.svg`). Binding in this trial: logo and name only; colours and typefaces are open (user, 2026-10-26).
- Voice: Italian, "tu" to the public, competent but welcoming, no exclamation marks, no emoji, never the word "banda", nothing folkloristic. No emphasis on a conductor figure.
- Motto: «Custodi della tradizione, interpreti del futuro». Eighteenth-century motto: «Le dissonanze in un accordo solo».

## Product Principles

1. Show people playing, not an institution talking.
2. Joining must look possible for a beginner or someone who stopped: say what it costs and that instruments are provided.
3. History is a credential, not the headline.
4. Every claim on the page is true and checkable.
